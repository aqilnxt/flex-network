# Flex Network — Design System

## Colors
- Primary: #2447F9 (Royal Blue)
- Accent: #459CE8 (Light Blue)
- Background: #FFFFFF, #F7F8FC, #F3F6FF
- Text: #0D0907 (Primary), #34364A (Secondary)
- Semantic: #22C55E (Success), #EF4444 (Error), #F59E0B (Warning)

## Typography
- Font: Inter
- Weights: 400 (Body), 500 (Nav), 600 (Buttons), 700 (Headings), 800 (Hero)

## Spacing Scale
- 4px, 8px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px, 64px

## Principles
- Clean, Modern, Approachable.
- White is the space. Blue is the brand and action.
